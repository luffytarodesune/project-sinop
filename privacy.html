Privacy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SINOP accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the application, including when you sign in with Google and enable Google Drive synchronization.

Effective: September 1, 2026Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. About SINOP

SINOP is a personal finance application for tracking income, accounts, recurring commitments, spending, savings, investments, goals, IOUs, KKB activity, grocery planning, reports, forecasts, and related financial information.

The current public web version is delivered as a static website. SINOP does not operate its own server-side database for your financial records. Your working data is stored in your browser, and, when you authorize cloud synchronization, a copy is stored in your own Google Drive application-data area.

2. Information SINOP accesses

Google account information

When you sign in with Google, SINOP may receive your Google account identifier, name, email address, and profile picture when available. This is used to identify the connected Google account, separate local workspaces by account, display the signed-in account, and associate cloud synchronization with the correct account.

Google Drive application data

If you authorize Google Drive synchronization, SINOP requests the drive.appdata permission. This is used only to create, read, update, and manage SINOP's own private application data in your Google Drive application-data area.

SINOP does not request access to your ordinary Google Drive files. It does not use the full Drive permission to browse your normal documents, photos, spreadsheets, shared files, or other Drive content.

Financial and planning information

Information you enter may include account balances, income, salary information and deductions, recurring commitments, expenses, savings, investments, goals, credit-card information you choose to record, IOUs, KKB entries, grocery information, notes, forecasts, and settings.

Synchronization metadata

SINOP may store a device identifier, cloud file identifier, schema version, revision number, timestamps, synchronization status, and the last known connected Google account identifier for sync and conflict prevention.

3. How SINOP uses information

  • displaying and calculating your personal finance records;
  • calculating budgets, balances, commitments, forecasts, goals, and reports;
  • identifying the Google account connected to SINOP;
  • saving and restoring your SINOP data between your own devices;
  • detecting synchronization conflicts and preventing accidental overwrites;
  • preserving app preferences and account-specific local workspaces; and
  • maintaining the security and reliability of the application.

SINOP does not use Google user data or your financial data to build advertising profiles, serve personalized advertising, sell data, or train advertising systems.

5. Sharing and disclosure

SINOP does not sell your personal information or financial data. It does not intentionally share your Google user data or financial records with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. Google processes information only as necessary to provide Google Sign-In, OAuth authorization, and Google Drive storage that you choose to use, subject to Google's own terms and privacy practices.

If a future feature involves sharing data with another person, such as collaborative KKB, SINOP will require an explicit user action and this Privacy Policy will be updated before such sharing is enabled.

6. Google API data use

SINOP's use of information received from Google APIs is limited to the user-facing functionality described in this Privacy Policy. The Google Drive application-data permission is used solely to synchronize and restore SINOP's own application data for the signed-in user. SINOP does not use Google API data for advertising, credit eligibility, surveillance, or unrelated analytics.

7. Retention and deletion

Local SINOP data remains on a device until it is cleared by you, removed by browser/site-data controls, replaced through a restore action, or otherwise removed through SINOP's data-management functions. Cloud SINOP data remains in your Google Drive application-data area until it is deleted through SINOP, removed through your Google account/app-access controls, or otherwise deleted from Google's application-data storage.

Signing out does not automatically delete your financial records.

8. Your choices and controls

  • grant or revoke Google authorization;
  • sign out or switch Google accounts;
  • revoke SINOP's Google access through your Google Account permissions;
  • use SINOP's export/backup functions where available;
  • clear local browser data; and
  • stop using SINOP at any time.

9. Security

SINOP is designed to minimize access to external services. Google access tokens are not intentionally written into SINOP's financial JSON or permanently stored as part of your finance data. Cloud synchronization uses Google's OAuth authorization and the limited Google Drive application-data permission.

No web application can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting access to your device, browser profile, and Google account.

10. Children's privacy

SINOP is not designed specifically for children. If you are not legally permitted to authorize online services or financial-data storage on your own, use SINOP only with the involvement of a parent, guardian, or other authorized adult where required.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SINOP's features, storage architecture, Google API permissions, or data practices change. The date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are published.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning SINOP, use the developer/support contact associated with SINOP's Google OAuth consent screen or contact the project maintainer through the SINOP project repository.
